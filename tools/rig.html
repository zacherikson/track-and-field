Sprite Rigger: drawn body parts → a game sprite sheet

  1. Drop in your art: one or more parts sheets (parts apart from each other), or one image per part. A plain white, green or checkerboard background is removed.
  2. Pick each part's name; its joints are guessed. Parts you don't need (labels, specks) can stay unnamed.
  3. Check each part's pivot joint and end joint, and click to move them. The game fits the part between them.
  4. Optional: Cut off what shouldn't show past a joint (a cut-off end over the shorts, a second wristband): click where to cut, before the pivot or past the end.
  5. Check the preview, then download <name>.png and <name>.json into src/athletes/sprites/.
