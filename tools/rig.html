Sprite Rigger: drawn body parts → a game sprite sheet

  1. Drop in a parts sheet (one image with every part, apart from each other) or one image per part. A plain white or checkerboard background is removed.
  2. Pick each part's name. Parts you don't need (labels, specks) can stay unnamed.
  3. For each part, click its pivot joint, then its end joint. The game fits the part between them.
  4. Check the preview, then download <name>.png and <name>.json into src/athletes/sprites/.
