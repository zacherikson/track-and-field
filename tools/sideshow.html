The home screens' sideshow skits (src/scenes/home/sideshow.js)

Every skit, one per row, on a strip of track. Serve the repo and open /tools/sideshow.html. Drag the slider to scrub, or tick Play. Options: ?t=3.2 (a moment), ?mirror (play them the other way round), ?skit=bodySlam (just one), ?h=110 (figure height, px).
